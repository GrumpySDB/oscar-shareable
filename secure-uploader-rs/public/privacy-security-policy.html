Privacy and Security Policy

Back to Uploader

This application was designed with user privacy and security as a top priority.


Aside from the data you choose to submit to the service, no data is collected, stored, or retained. No data is ever distributed or sold to any third parties. No IP addresses are ever collected or retained. If you wish to remove previously uploaded data at any time, please use the delete function provided within the service, or reach out to the administrator who gave you access.

Your SD card data does not contain personally identifiable information. The only unique identifier in your SD Card data is your CPAP machine’s serial number. Your OSCAR profile is created automatically for you with bare minimum information (your username) and that is all. Do not input any extra information to your profile that you do not wish to share.

This service is delivered through Cloudflare tunneling. All data transmitted to and from the service is protected using SSL/TLS encryption while in transit. By design Cloudflare terminates TLS encryption and then re-encrypts it again before forwarding it to the uploader service. As such, your data is encrypted while in transit and briefly unencrypted by Cloudflare before being encrypted in transit again. If this concerns you, to mitigate this you have the option to enable "Tinfoil Hat Mode" to ensure your data is never fully unencrypted until it reaches its destination - this sharing service.

No Google browser analytics or any other data collection is in place by us. Cloudflare may have its own policies with respect to data traveling through their tunnel system. We have disabled any traffic analytics offered by Cloudflare to protect user privacy.

This service was built with a strong emphasis on application security. Multiple safeguards and hardening measures have been implemented to reduce risk and protect user data. However, no web application can be guaranteed to be completely secure against all possible threats.

Be aware that portions of this software were created using generative AI and therefore could be subject to unexpected behaviour. Thorough efforts have been undertaken to ensure everything is working and behaving as designed.

By using this service, you agree to use it responsibly and not upload any content that is illegal, harmful, or inappropriate.
