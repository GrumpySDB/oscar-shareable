The uploader currently supports various Resmed Airsense 10 and 11 CPAP devices. It also supports Wellue / Viatom oximetry data as well as Emay oximetry data.

As the developer does not have access to data samples from other models to test with, the uploader cannot yet support any other devices. Please reach out if you want to assist in getting support for your device!

You may be selecting the wrong folder/location. OSCAR is a bit picky in how you select your data folder.

If you are uploading from a supported Resmed device, ensure that you are selecting the entire ROOT of your SD Card folder (so the entire SD card). Do not upload ONLY the DATALOG folder! The script will process the SD card contents locally in your browser, and it will only upload to the server the appropriate files necessary. It will not upload your entire SD Card, only the necessary new files within the selected date range.

If you are uploading a wellue/viatom oximetry device data, look for the folder that contains the db_o2.db file and a numbered folder. You can select the numbered folder.

For Emay data you can select the folder containing your .spo2 files.

In most cases on most modern devices, it shouldn't be drastically different in terms of performance than OSCAR running on your home computer. However, some additional latency is expected due to the nature of running the application remotely from a VNC server and delivering to your web browser. Its not possible to be completely latency free in this current implementation.

However, you can tweak some of the display settings to see if it might help display it faster or better on your particular device. Click on the blue bar on the left side of the screen, and play around with display settings, screen settings, etc until you find something that works for you. You can also adjust screen resolution, UI scaling, anti-aliasing etc if you are having visual problems. I recommend applying "Use CSS cursors" because it helps OSCAR feel a little more responsive to your cursor movement.

The application is hosted in Montreal, Canada so the further away you are, the worse the latency may be. Its hosted on a modestly spec-ed VPS, so if there are multiple users accessing the service, the experience may degrade somewhat. Running OSCAR in a VPS requires a lot of compute resources compared to most web services, and high end hosting is not cheap. It is a free service (for you) which costs the developer money to make available, so please be patient.

Tinfoil Hat Mode adds application-layer encryption in your browser before files are uploaded. Files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and the per-file key is wrapped with the uploader server's RSA public key. This means intermediaries only see ciphertext.

Why do this if HTTPS already exists?

Cloudflare-terminated TLS protects traffic on the network, but Cloudflare can still see plaintext while proxying content. Application-layer encryption helps address that shortcoming by keeping upload contents encrypted until they reach the uploader origin, where decryption happens server-side.

Is this required?

Not really required. Resmed SD card files used by OSCAR generally do not contain personal identity data. This extra encryption is optional, provided as a courtesy, and included because it is a fun proof-of-concept.

Local accounts are offered as a privacy-first option for users who wish to remain fully anonymous instead of linking a third-party login like Discord.

It is an intentional choice by the developer not to collect or store your email address. Instead, a highly secure password and a recovery phrase are generated for you automatically upon signup. By generating a password for you, the application ensures you cannot accidentally reuse a password you have used in another application, which protects your privacy and security.

Because no email address is collected, there is no traditional "forgot password" reset email option. If you forget or lose your password and your recovery phrase, you must contact the administrator to have them manually reset your password.

Because we do not collect email addresses, if you lose your generated password AND your recovery phrase, you will not be able to log in. To reset your password, you must contact the site administrator and explain the situation. They will be able to generate a new password for you.
